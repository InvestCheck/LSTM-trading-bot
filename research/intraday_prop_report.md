# Intraday trendline engine, prop firm version (flat at the close, re entry at the open)

Trades from research_intraday.py (long window) cut at the last bar ending by 16:45 ET of each session and re entered at the next 18:00 ET open while the engine still holds; each leg is one day's P&L with its own costs. Instrument set and entry hours fixed on exploration: median cost <= 0.1R, flat net avgR >= -0.05 (hours need 100+ legs). Holdout 2017 to 2026 is a second look (the main study already used it).
